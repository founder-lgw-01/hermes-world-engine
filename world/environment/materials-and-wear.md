# Materials and wear

## Rules
- Arches, columns, and paving use dense marble or limestone, worn by mechanical fracturing, vines, moss, and dark moisture stains. [stated: interview q5]
- Giant books and folders use paper or parchment, worn by moisture rippling, torn and frayed edges, and yellowing/browning. [stated: interview q5]
- Helmet, armor, and staff use forged metallic alloys, worn by scratches, dents, oxidation, rust, or patina. [stated: interview q5]
- Vegetation is living plant tissue that grows over, roots into, and slowly breaks down static materials. [stated: interview q5]

## Exclusions
- No pristine plastic, glossy consumer-tech surface, or futuristic energy-glass material appears. [stated: interview q5; interview q9]

## Open
- The exact metal alloy names are unknown. [stated: interview q5]

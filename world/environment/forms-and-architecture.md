# Forms and architecture

## Rules
- Roman rounded arches, fluted cylindrical columns, triangular pediments, and spiraling portal vortices recur in architecture. [stated: interview q6]
- Cracked rectangular and square paving stones and rigid geometric folder/books recur in the landscape. [stated: interview q6]
- Oval and teardrop leaves and fractal branching trees/vines recur in vegetation. [stated: interview q6]
- Allowed fantastical vehicles are flying chariots, Vedic flying craft, and magic carpets, provided they remain classical/magical rather than mechanical or science-fictional. [stated: interview q6; interview q9]

## Exclusions
- No crystalline spires, non-classical metallic structures, or mechanical craft appear. [stated: interview q9]

## Open
- The visual form and operation of permitted magical vehicles are unknown. [stated: interview q6]

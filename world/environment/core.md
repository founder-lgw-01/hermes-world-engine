# Core world rules

## Rules
- The world is called Hermetic Gates. [stated: interview q1]
- It fuses classical mythology with digital directory structures: Hermes carries information across a network of physicalized folders, portals, and routes. [stated: interview q2]
- It is a mythological operating system in which agents travel through data pathways, folders, and retrieval portals. [stated: interview q3]

## Exclusions
- No neon blue, cyan, magenta, bright red, grass green, or other vivid primary/secondary color appears in the default world palette. [stated: interview q4]
- No futuristic or science-fiction technology, mechanical spacecraft, hover vehicles, jet propulsion, or advanced machinery appears. [stated: interview q9]
- No alien, crystalline, or non-classical metallic architecture appears. [stated: interview q9]
- No floating terrain, suspended waterfalls, outer-space sky, visible galaxy, colorful starfield, modern attire, futuristic armor, or mechanical suit appears. [stated: interview q9]
- Architecture remains grounded in classical stone, marble, Greek/Roman columns, and arches. [stated: interview q9]

## Open
- The lower-world geography and boundaries beyond the named provisional locations are unknown. [stated: interview q7]

# Palette and light

## Rules
- Dominant colors are charcoal, slate grey, and antique sepia in matte aged-stone, shadow, and ink-engraving tones. [stated: interview q4]
- Luminous gold, warm amber, and ethereal yellow are reserved for active data pathways, portals, and glowing folder information. [stated: interview q4]
- Dawn is a fragile rose-gold wash that reaches high points before lower pavilions. [stated: interview q8]
- Midday is direct and prism-like, with hard-edged shadows under heavy pediments. [stated: interview q8]
- Dusk is amber, orange, and gold with long cinematic shadows. [stated: interview q8]
- Night uses grounded heavenly light and warm pale-gold wayfinding luminosity; colored cosmic nebulae and icy-blue city light are excluded. [stated: interview q8; interview q9]
- Aether-mists may rise from waterfalls and catch ambient warm light. [stated: interview q8]
- Harmless prismatic crystalline frost is an unresolved weather concept because full-spectrum color and crystalline elements are excluded. [stated: interview q8; interview q9]

## Exclusions
- No artificial cool-tone or full-spectrum light appears. [stated: interview q4; interview q9]
- Gold is the sole indicator of active power and data. [stated: interview q4]

## Open
- The permitted visual treatment of prismatic frost needs reconciliation with the palette and crystal exclusions. [stated: interview q8; interview q9]

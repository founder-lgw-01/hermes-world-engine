# Hermetic Gates, style rules

## Medium
- Medium: Digital illustration with traditional Japanese woodblock-print (ukiyo-e) stylization, distinct line art, and flat color blocking. [stated: interview q11]
- Finish: Matte textured paper with an aged, speckled parchment background and vintage/antiqued character. [stated: interview q11]

## Look
Antiquarian, woodblock-inspired, Hermetic, monumental, weathered, gold-lit. [stated: interview q12]

## Camera defaults
- Lens: 28mm full-frame equivalent. [stated: interview q13]
- Camera height: Slightly below eye level; high overlook is an explicit exception. [stated: interview q13]
- Distance: Medium-wide/wide; focal object remains readable inside substantial environment. [stated: interview q13]
- Depth of field: Deep focus; shallow focus is an explicit intimate/secretive-moment exception. [stated: interview q13]

## Aspect ratio
- Default: 16:9. [stated: interview q14]
- Also allowed: 4:5, 1:1, 3:2; 9:16 only when explicitly requested for vertical/mobile motion. [stated: interview q14]

## Framing conventions
- Headroom: Preserve helmet, wings, halo when applicable, and monument scale. [inferred]
- Where characters sit in frame: Lower left/right third facing route space; centered symmetry only for gateway/icon moments. [stated: interview q15]
- How much environment shows: 60–70% by default; full-/three-quarter-body where location matters. [stated: interview q15]

## Exclusions
- The image holds no readable lettering, captions, logos, watermarks, UI, code, file paths, brand marks, borders, collages, device screens, or presentation frames. [stated: interview q16]
- The image holds no neon blue, cyan, magenta, vivid red outside Hermes’s ceremonial ascendant state, grass green, futuristic/science-fiction technology, crystals, floating terrain, cosmic sky, modern clothing, or generic superhero design. [stated: interview q4, q9, q27]

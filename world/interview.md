# Interview, the raw record

## Part 1, the world

1. **World name:** Hermetic Gates.
2. **Era/technology:** Classical mythology intersecting digital directory structures; Hermes is a high-speed routing/fetch agent, folders are physical architecture, and portals are directory navigation.
3. **Place:** A mythological operating system where agents travel through data pathways, folders, and retrieval portals.
4. **Palette:** Dominant charcoal, slate grey, antique sepia; accents luminous gold, warm amber, ethereal yellow; banned neon blue, cyan, magenta, bright red, grass green, and vivid primary/secondary colors.
5. **Materials/wear:** Marble/limestone cracks and gathers vines, moss, and stains; parchment/paper ripples, tears, frays, and yellows; forged metal scratches, dents, oxidizes, and patinas; vegetation overtakes static materials.
6. **Forms:** Roman arches, fluted columns, triangular pediments, spiral portals, rectilinear paving/folders/books, oval/teardrop leaves, and fractal vines/trees; magical vehicles may be flying chariots, Vedic flying craft, or magic carpets.
7. **Locations:** Gateway Pavilion—temple-like pediment, distant golden sunlight, aged stone blocks. Observatory Court—circular platform, setting sun, polished black marble with gold celestial charts. Crystal Citadel—initial crystal/cool-light concept, unresolved because excluded. Sky-Bridges and Passages—initial floating/cool-light bridge concept, unresolved because excluded. Aether Falls and Islands—initial temple/waterfall/floating-island concept; only grounded temple, earth, stone, mist, and warm-gold light remain potentially compatible.
8. **Daily light/weather:** Dawn rose-gold; midday direct/prism-like with hard shadows; dusk amber/orange/gold with long shadows; night grounded heavenly light and warm-gold pathways. Aether-mists may rise from waterfalls. Prismatic frost is unresolved under crystal/palette exclusions.
9. **World exclusions:** No sci-fi technology, alien/crystalline architecture, floating terrain, cosmic backgrounds, modern/sci-fi attire, full-spectrum gradients, or saturated hues.
10. **World references:** `gateway-courtyard-01.png` and `gateway-courtyard-02.png` directly control palette, materials, classical-directory architecture, and Gateway Pavilion. `landscape-scale-inspiration.jpg` is scale inspiration only; it cannot transfer palette, crystals, floating terrain, cool light, cosmic sky, sci-fi vehicles, or architecture.

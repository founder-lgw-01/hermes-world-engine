# 01_story, select or establish the reusable story foundation

One job: agree series-level story rules without rewriting world canon.

## Inputs
- Working: the selected approved `runs/studio/<production>/00-intake-N.md`,
  operator answers and the explicitly selected foundation version, if any.
- Reference: `../../_reference/studio-rules.md`,
  `../../_templates/story-foundation.md`; selected approved world records by
  exact path and their explicit dependencies, not a full world dump.

Do NOT load: raw world interviews, unrelated foundations, shot folders,
composition adapters or unadopted creative recommendations from the design.

## Process
1. Refuse unless the intake says `status: approved` and selects narrative work.
2. If an existing approved foundation fits, reuse its exact version. Do not
   generate a replacement to satisfy a numbered-stage checklist.
3. Otherwise interview for series scope, genre/exclusions and recurring conflict.
   Reference world time/geography/environment rather than restating those facts.
   The production's audience and purpose stay in the intake. A new world fact
   is a setup preparation task, not something this stage may canonize.
4. Write a draft foundation from the template. Mark unresolved decisions under
   Open. Any departure from an approved foundation requires explicit approval
   and a new version, never an in-place edit of approved content.
5. Obtain human approval of the selected/new foundation before the sequence
   brief is derived. Pass the exact approved version to `../02_sequence/CONTEXT.md`.

## Outputs
- `stories/<series>/01-foundation-N.md` — new versions only when required.
- Reuse mode emits no replacement: the sequence's sources name the already
  approved foundation. No duplicate series facts or secondary status record.

## Human check
The foundation is `status: approved` only after approval of the named version.
Creative recommendations were actually adopted, not inferred. Its scope is
reusable; production-specific events and audience have not become world canon.
The next handoff names its exact path, hash and authoritative approval evidence.
